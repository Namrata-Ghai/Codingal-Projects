About My Character

Character Name: Blossom

Cartoon: Powerpuff Girls

Why I Like This Character

Blossom is smart, brave, and a natural leader.

She always uses clever plans and her ice breath to protect her sisters and save Townsville.

She teaches us about teamwork, responsibility, and solving problems with our brains.
